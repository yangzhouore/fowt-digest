## Phase 1 final research checkpoint

Research coverage runs from **1 January 2018 to 20 November 2026 inclusive**.
All 47 canonical Industry companies have a reviewed outcome for every annual
window. The final 17-company pass recorded 153 decisions, including 126 windows
with retained developments, 14 searches with no qualifying development, and
13 documented source limitations. The final roster has **14 COMPLETE**,
**33 COMPLETE_WITH_SOURCE_LIMITATION**, and **0 INCOMPLETE** histories. These
statuses describe the reviewed public evidence.

  limitation must be documented before marking a year U.

## Scope and method

The canonical universe is the 47 existing Industry IDs in
`web/data/industry/industry-map.ts`. Vestas began with 163 accepted events, all
of which are preserved. The other 46 companies entered discovery with zero
memberships in the event catalogue.

Discovery used role-specific publisher-domain queries across 2018–2020,
2021–2023 and 2024–20 November 2026, followed by counterparty, regulator,
programme and trade-source checks for selected candidates. The 46 domain
queries, each run in three date windows, surfaced 720 distinct result URLs
from the returned listings. This is a count of leads, including directories,
annual reports and incidental mentions, and is separate from editorial
decisions. Date windows label the search coverage. Search results are capped,
archives may be incomplete or inaccessible and indexing dates can differ from
publication dates. Querying a publisher’s domain does not establish that all
of its archives or every year was reviewed.

The review records decisions on 144 distinct publication URLs. Of these, 124
support a new event or an existing event enriched in this pass. The exclusion
register contains 22 decisions; two of those publications were retained for a
different development or company relationship.

Retained candidates were checked for a dated, distinct offshore or floating
wind development, an explicit material role for the linked entity and an
attributable publication. Existing Projects evidence was used to reconcile
canonical project identities. Where Engineering or Digital & AI already
retained the same publication, the catalogue uses that source identity.
Publication dates in the catalogue follow the lead citation. For the WindFloat
Atlantic mooring retrospective, the summary gives the earlier operations
window. Related-project links use the project catalogue and leave missing
projects out of `projectIds`.
