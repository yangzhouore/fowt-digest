companies that were open at the 587-event checkpoint. Review was completed on
23 November 2026. The prior dossiers retain the evidence for the other 30
canonical companies.

The work compared annual company and historical-name discovery with issuer
archives, project accounts, developer announcements, dated technical documents
and public records. It checked accepted event titles, dates, source URLs and
contract boundaries before assigning canonical IDs. Dates are drawn from the
published evidence. The year matrix uses **R** for qualifying developments
retained, **N** for searched without a qualifying development, and **U** for the
specified source or dated evidence unavailable. A company can close with source
limitations even when independent publications verify some activity in every
year. An editorially closed history is bounded by the public sources reviewed.
