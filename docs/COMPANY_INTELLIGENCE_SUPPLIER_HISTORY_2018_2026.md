# Company Intelligence supplier history audit

Research window 2018-01-01 through 2026-11-20 inclusive. This checkpoint reviews
eleven anchoring, mooring, cable and grid suppliers. Vestas source coverage,
the canonical Projects dataset, and the Company Intelligence schema and UI were
preserved.

Each company had nine open windows at the accepted 511-event checkpoint. The
pass used separate annual searches for each company, with historical company and
business unit names, followed by issuer archive, investor, product, project,
public programme or counterparty material. Dated event evidence determines the
publication date. Recent archive migrations, revised case studies, and project
retrospectives were checked against original dates where available.

- **R** means reviewed with a qualifying development retained.
- **N** means reviewed, with no separate qualifying development in the sources inspected.
- **U** means the relevant archive or stable dated evidence was unavailable. The individual row records the fallback source and what it could establish.
- Annual closure describes the evidence available in the reviewed public sources. Companies may have unpublished awards or inaccessible archives.
