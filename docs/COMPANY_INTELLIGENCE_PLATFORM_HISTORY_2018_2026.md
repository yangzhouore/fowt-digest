# Floating-platform company history and annual closure

Coverage window runs from 1 January 2018 through 20 November 2026, inclusive.
This review examined company indexes, annual listings and filings, specific
technology and project names, and developer, supplier, regulator or programme
records. Each of the 63 years below received a separate dated discovery search
and a narrower archive or project check. Broad search results sometimes returned
identical retrospective pages across years. Only the year-specific evidence was
used to close a window. The linked source in each annual row is a reproducible
decision point, and the shared catalogue lists the retained event publications.

A closure applies to evidence publicly located by the stated cutoff. A source
limitation remains visible when an old reporting route is missing or a primary
source provides only a year or conflicting stage dates. Undated portfolio
references alone do not establish an event date.
