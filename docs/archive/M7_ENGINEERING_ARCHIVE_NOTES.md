# M7 Engineering Briefing Archive Notes

## Coverage Approach

M7C creates representative static Engineering Briefing coverage from August 2025
through August 2026. It is not complete historical coverage.

Each edition is anchored to a real publication week containing at least one
accepted source-backed engineering item. When a calendar month did not have a
verified item in the reviewed source set, no filler item was created. Nearby
source-backed weeks were retained instead.

## Representative Substitutions

- April 2026 has no standalone edition in this prototype because the reviewed
  source set did not provide a suitable accepted floating offshore wind
  engineering item for that month. Nearby March and May 2026 editions provide
  representative continuity without fabricating coverage.
- Several months include more than one edition where multiple source-backed
  engineering developments were available.

## Boundary

The archive uses manual static source records under `web/data/briefings/`.
It does not add scraping, source APIs, scheduled collection, AI generation,
backend services, databases, or Research Digest pipeline changes.
